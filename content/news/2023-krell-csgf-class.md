---
title: "DOE CSGF Strengthens Diversity With 2023-2024 Class"
venue: Krell Institute
url: https://www.krellinst.org/csgf/about-doe-csgf/news-events/2023-incoming-class
---

The Department of Energy Computational Science Graduate Fellowship (DOE CSGF) will receive 39 new students for 2023-24, a new class size record. The incoming fellows will attend 25 universities across the country as they learn to apply high-performance computing (HPC) to research across a variety of fields, including computer science, astrophysics, electrical engineering, applied mathematics, and environmental fluid mechanics.
