Krell Institute

The Department of Energy Computational Science Graduate Fellowship (DOE CSGF) will

will attend 25 universities across the country as they learn to apply high-performance
computing (HPC) to research across a variety of fields, including computer science,
astrophysics, electrical engineering, applied mathematics, and environmental fluid
